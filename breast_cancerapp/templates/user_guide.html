Image Analysis
How to Use:
  1. Navigate to "AI Analysis Tools" "Image Analysis"
  2. Upload breast cancer histopathology images
  3. Supported formats: JPEG, PNG, BMP, TIFF
  4. Click "Analyze Image" to get AI prediction
Results Include:
  • Prediction Benign (0) or Malignant (1)
  • Confidence Percentage accuracy score
  • Stage Cancer stage assessment
  • Probabilities Detailed breakdown

Risk Assessment
How to Use:
  1. Navigate to "AI Analysis Tools" "Risk Assessment"
  2. Fill in patient demographic information
  3. Include medical history and genetic factors
  4. Click "Predict" to get risk evaluation
Required Information:
  • Age Patient age and menstrual history
  • Family Family cancer history
  • Genetic BRCA mutation status
  • Biopsy Previous biopsy results
